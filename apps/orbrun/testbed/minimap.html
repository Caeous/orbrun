Facing
The same scene and the same renderer, drawn twice: on the left the ground snaps to the nearest compass quarter, on the right it stands the way the player faces. Q / E or the buttons turn; on a compass facing the two agree, on a diagonal they are 45 degrees apart. The orange target cursor is painted on the ground the way the app's features and highlights are: upright at a compass facing, lying with the ground over the 45 degrees of a diagonal. The player's white mark stands in its cell instead, upright whatever the ground does, as the monsters and items do in the app. It sits three cells along the quarter heading an aim's keys are read against, so it lands on the same cell on both maps. Served by the dev server at /testbed/minimap.html; not part of the build.
